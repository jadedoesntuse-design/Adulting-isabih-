# Multiple-choice self-assessment: Which areas of adulting do I need help with?

Instructions: For each question, pick the option (A–D) that best describes you right now. This is a non-interactive self-reflection — there are no right or wrong answers. After you finish, look for patterns: which topics keep coming up? Those are the areas to prioritize.

1. Which area do you most want help with right now?
   A) Managing money and budgeting
   B) Finding or keeping housing
   C) Job search, resume, or workplace skills
   D) Health, insurance, or medical care

2. How confident are you at creating and sticking to a monthly budget?
   A) Very confident — I track everything
   B) Somewhat confident — I have a rough plan
   C) Not confident — I try but struggle
   D) Not at all — I don’t have a budget

3. Which best describes your knowledge of taxes and filing?
   A) I file confidently and understand the basics
   B) I know the basics but use help sometimes
   C) I’m unsure and avoid it if possible
   D) I have no idea where to start

4. When it comes to housing, which applies to you?
   A) I own or plan to buy soon
   B) I rent and understand lease terms
   C) I’m new to renting / unsure about leases
   D) I’m experiencing housing instability

5. How comfortable are you cooking basic, healthy meals?
   A) Very comfortable — I cook often
   B) I can cook simple meals most days
   C) I struggle to cook consistently
   D) I rarely cook; I rely on takeout

6. How do you feel about managing your schedule and commitments?
   A) I have systems and a reliable routine
   B) I’m generally organized but slip at times
   C) I often miss things or feel overwhelmed
   D) I don’t have a routine or reliable system

7. How confident are you dealing with medical care and insurance?
   A) I know how to use my insurance and find care
   B) I mostly manage but need reminders
   C) I’m confused about coverage and billing
   D) I don’t have insurance or regular care

8. How comfortable are you reading and signing contracts or legal documents?
   A) I review and understand terms before signing
   B) I understand some terms or ask for help
   C) I need help interpreting contracts
   D) I avoid signing or don’t know where to start

9. What best describes your current job situation?
   A) Employed with a stable position and skills I use
   B) Employed but looking to improve skills or switch
   C) Actively job searching and need help with applications
   D) Not currently working and need basic job-search help

10. How would you rate your mental-health supports and coping tools?
   A) Good supports and healthy coping strategies
   B) Some supports; I could use more resources
   C) I struggle and want better help/resources
   D) I’m in crisis or need immediate support
